\subsection{Link-aware attention masks}
The four mask conditions, ordered by attention permitted
($\doccausal < \crossdoc \le \concatlink \le \concat$):
\begin{description}
  \item[\doccausal] block-diagonal causal; documents fully isolated (baseline).
  \item[\crossdoc] when $A$ links to $B$ and both are packed, tokens in $A$ from the
        link position onward attend to all of $B$; asymmetric, causal order kept.
  \item[\concatlink] compute control: grant from the \emph{entire} source document,
        not just the link position (same connectivity, more attention).
  \item[\concat] compute control: connected components merged into one causal
        super-document (most attention, no inference-time linking).
\end{description}
