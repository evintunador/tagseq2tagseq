\paragraph{The effect is a genuine linking bias.}
The placebo and leakage controls (Section~\ref{sec:results}) rule out the two deflationary
readings of the cross-document $\Delta$nll. The derangement placebo removes almost all of
the gain while holding the amount of granted context fixed, so the benefit is specificity
to the correct linked document, not generic extra context; and the reduction persists in
the lowest $n$-gram-overlap stratum, so it is not verbatim copying of duplicated text. What
remains is the intended inductive bias: attention gated onto the right cross-document
target at generation time.
